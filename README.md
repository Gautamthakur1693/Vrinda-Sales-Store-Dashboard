# 📊 Vrinda Store Sales Dashboard (Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Vrinda Store Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze annual sales performance for the year **2022**. The dashboard provides valuable business insights through charts, KPIs, slicers, and pivot tables, helping stakeholders understand sales trends, customer behavior, and order performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze annual sales performance.
- Compare sales and orders across different months.
- Understand customer demographics (Age & Gender).
- Identify top-performing sales channels.
- Analyze order status and delivery performance.
- Find the top revenue-generating states.
- Enable dynamic filtering using slicers.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Orders vs Sales
- Monthly comparison of total orders and sales amount.
- Identifies seasonal sales trends.

### 2. Sales by Gender
- Percentage contribution of Men vs Women customers.
- Helps understand customer demographics.

### 3. Order Status
- Delivered
- Cancelled
- Returned
- Refunded

### 4. Top 5 States
Shows highest revenue-generating states:
- Maharashtra
- Karnataka
- Uttar Pradesh
- Telangana
- Tamil Nadu

### 5. Orders by Age & Gender
Customer segmentation based on:
- Adults
- Teenagers
- Seniors

with separate comparison for Men and Women.

### 6. Orders by Sales Channel
Sales distribution across platforms:
- Amazon
- Myntra
- Flipkart
- Ajio
- Meesho
- Nalli
- Others

### 7. Interactive Filters (Slicers)
Users can filter dashboard using:
- Month
- Sales Channel
- Product Category

---

## 📌 Key Insights

- ✅ Women customers contribute **64%** of total sales.
- ✅ **92%** of orders were successfully delivered.
- ✅ Amazon is the highest-performing sales channel (**35%**).
- ✅ Maharashtra generated the highest sales (**2.99M**).
- ✅ Adult women are the largest customer segment.
- ✅ Sales peaked around **March** and gradually declined towards the end of the year.
